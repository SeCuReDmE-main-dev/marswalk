[USGS projection documentation](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/docs/concepts/camera-geometry-and-projections/learning-about-map-projections/), [USGS spherical Mars DTM metadata example](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/ctx_digital_terrain_model_of_mars_insight_landing_site_ellipse_9_far_east).

I use Horn's original paper as the reference for the weighted local gradient method requested in the audit. I distinguish that estimator from the implemented two-point baselines; I do not claim that either estimator alone validates astronaut-scale hazards. [Horn, 1981, author-hosted paper](https://people.csail.mit.edu/bkph/papers/Hill-Shading.pdf).

I use the HiRISE team's guidance to distinguish image pixel scale from DTM post spacing and terrain precision. Its overview says DTM terrain is generally derived at post spacing about four times the input image pixel scale. That does not independently establish a universal “3–4 DTM posts” detectability bound for this particular blended raster or validate the chosen 0.45 m horizontal margin. I leave those science-specific claims unverified. [HiRISE DTM overview](https://hirise.lpl.arizona.edu/dtm/about.php).

I have not established equivalence of this custom calibration to the published error-guarantee interpretation.
