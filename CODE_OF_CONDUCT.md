# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`)

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and teammates of the **MarsWalk** project for the 2026 NASA International Space Apps Challenge pledge to make participation in our project a harassment-free, constructive, and scientifically rigorous experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy, respect, and clear communication under hackathon time constraints
- Respecting domain ownership (`frontend/` creative freedom and `src/marswalk/` scientific & contract rigor)
- Being transparent about data provenance (`MEASURED`, `DERIVED`, `SIMULATED`) and AI tool usage
- Focusing on what is best for the team, the scientific validity of the project, and the Space Apps community

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via GitHub Issues or Space Apps team channels.
